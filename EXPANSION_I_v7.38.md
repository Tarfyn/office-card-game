# Office Card Game — Alpha Card Pool Expansion I v7.38

Expansion I converts the v7.37 content-gap audit into a focused 10-card test batch. It does not change starter lists, format limits, economy values, keywords or engine effect families.

## New cards

### Customer Service
- **CS-021 Triage Agent** — Cost 1 / Power 1 Staff, Ticket + Call. Straightforward early bridge body.
- **CS-022 Warm Transfer** — Cost 1 Action, Ticket + Call. +1 Power to a Customer Service Employee; +2 total if the target carries both Ticket and Call.

### IT
- **IT-021 Rollback Plan** — Cost 1 Fix + Deploy Action. Returns an IT System from Archive to hand, but it cannot be replayed that turn.

### Office
- **OFC-016 Receptionist** — Cost 1 / Power 1 Staff, Email + Meeting. First Email/Meeting play each turn can cycle the top card to the bottom.
- **OFC-017 Meeting Minutes** — Cost 1 Meeting + Process Action. Recycles an Office Meeting/Process Action from Archive into the Deck, then draws 1.

### Marketing
- **MKT-016 Damage Control** — Cost 1 Campaign + Content Incident. When a Marketing Employee would be destroyed by battle, recover a Campaign/Content Action from Archive.
- **MKT-017 Creative Sync** — Cost 2 Campaign + Content System. First Campaign Action each turn after Content activity gives a top-2 ordering decision.

### Production
- **PRD-016 Emergency Shutdown** — Cost 1 Machine Incident. A targeted opponent effect aimed at a Production Machine System can be answered by returning that System to hand.
- **PRD-017 Quality Hold** — Cost 1 Output Action. Archive one of your Production Staff Employees, then draw 2; deliberate board-for-hand conversion instead of more raw Power.

### Neutral
- **N-015 Shared Inbox** — Cost 2 Email System. First Email Action each turn costs 1 less.

## Gap impact
- Card pool grows **97 → 107**.
- Customer Service native Cost≤2 Employee definitions: **2 → 3**.
- Office native Cost≤2 Employee definitions: **2 → 3**.
- Marketing native Incident definitions: **1 → 2**.
- Production native Incident definitions: **1 → 2**.
- Office / Marketing / Production remain intentionally below the v7.37 18-card pool-depth threshold at 17 each; a later expansion can deepen them without bloating this first batch.
- Marketing still has only two native Cost≤2 Employee definitions; that remains a valid follow-up signal rather than being silently papered over.

## Guardrails
- No starter deck changes in v7.38.
- No card-rule changes to the original 97 definitions.
- No new effect type, keyword, timing window or format rule.
- Existing T0–T3 sandbox rarity derivation and craft/shred values are unchanged.
- New cards intentionally ship without bespoke artwork and use the existing fallback-art pipeline until final artwork is produced.
