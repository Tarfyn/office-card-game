# Artwork Test Set — 6 cards

Use all six to establish one coherent visual language before generating the wider catalog.

## 1. CS-001 — Customer Service Agent

- Type: Employee
- Department: Customer Service
- `artId`: `alpha-samples/customer-service-agent`
- Visual idea: capable customer service employee at a desk with headset, multiple incoming calls/tickets visible through props/screens; composed expression despite mild office chaos.
- Tone: relatable, lightly satirical, not miserable or caricatured.
- Key read: “I can take a look at that.”

## 2. IT-003 — System Administrator

- Type: Employee
- Department: IT
- `artId`: `alpha-samples/system-administrator`
- Visual idea: sysadmin in a server/IT workspace calmly maintaining several systems while everyone else would consider the situation intimidating; coffee or practical IT clutter can support the scene.
- Tone: competent, understated IT humor rather than hacker/cyberpunk cliché.
- Key read: infrastructure person who quietly keeps everything running.

## 3. OFC-007 — Approval Required

- Type: Incident
- Department: Office
- `artId`: `alpha-samples/approval-required`
- Visual idea: a simple request/document trapped in an absurdly long approval chain — stacks of folders, stamps, signatures, waiting hands, or a bureaucratic desk obstacle course.
- Tone: recognizable corporate bureaucracy with a visual punchline.
- Key read: nothing moves until somebody approves it.

## 4. MKT-012 — Going Viral

- Type: Action
- Department: Marketing
- `artId`: `alpha-samples/going-viral`
- Visual idea: marketing/social team watching a piece of content suddenly explode in attention; phones/screens/activity indicators may be abstracted but avoid readable platform branding or logos.
- Tone: energetic, slightly chaotic, triumphant.
- Key read: sudden runaway reach/attention.

## 5. PRD-008 — Plant Manager

- Type: Employee — Executive
- Department: Production
- `artId`: `alpha-samples/plant-manager`
- Visual idea: confident plant manager overlooking a busy production floor/assembly line, visibly focused on output and throughput; employees/machines create depth behind the central figure.
- Tone: commanding workplace satire, not villainous.
- Key read: “I want full output.”

## 6. N-013 — Coffee Machine

- Type: System — Neutral
- Department: Neutral
- `artId`: `alpha-samples/coffee-machine`
- Visual idea: office coffee machine treated with comically disproportionate importance — employees gathering around it like critical infrastructure, perhaps during a stressful morning.
- Tone: warm office-culture joke; visually distinct from employee portraits.
- Key read: “Business-critical infrastructure.”

## Shared prompt suffix for all six

Use the same wording in every generation for the style portion, for example:

> Cohesive modern office-satire trading card artwork, polished editorial illustration, contemporary workplace, expressive but believable characters, cinematic yet natural office lighting, clean readable composition, medium detail, same fictional company universe, no text, no logos, no card frame, no UI, 16:9 composition, main subject in central safe area.

Keep that style suffix identical and only change the card-specific scene description.
