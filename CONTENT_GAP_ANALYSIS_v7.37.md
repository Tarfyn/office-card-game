# Office Card Game — Content Gap Analysis v7.37

This pass intentionally does **not** add cards yet. It converts the current 97-card Alpha pool into objective content-depth signals so the first expansion answers actual gaps instead of chasing a round card count.

## Current department pool depth
- Customer Service: 20 cards — 7 Employees / 5 Actions / 5 Incidents / 3 Systems.
- IT: 19 cards — 8 Employees / 6 Actions / 2 Incidents / 3 Systems.
- Office: 15 cards — 6 Employees / 4 Actions / 3 Incidents / 2 Systems.
- Marketing: 15 cards — 7 Employees / 5 Actions / 1 Incident / 2 Systems.
- Production: 15 cards — 8 Employees / 3 Actions / 1 Incident / 3 Systems.

## Objective gaps carried into Expansion I
1. **Office pool depth** — 15 cards, plus only two native Employees at Cost 2 or less. Add a low-cost Office body/utility piece before adding another expensive payoff.
2. **Marketing reactive depth** — only one native Incident. Add a second reactive tool that supports Campaign/Content rather than raw generic negation.
3. **Production reactive depth** — only one native Incident, while the starter remains the clearest heuristic power/ease-of-piloting watch item. Add interaction/decision texture, not more raw board Power.
4. **Marketing / Production / Office pool depth** — each sits at 15 department cards versus 19–20 for the two currently deepest departments.
5. **Customer Service early variety** — the v7.34 starter fix improved its CS-vs-IT result, but the department still has only two native Cost≤2 Employee definitions. Add a straightforward early Ticket/Call bridge rather than another reactive trick.

## Explicit non-gaps
- The major engine pairs already have native bridge cards: Ticket+Call, System+Deploy, Process+Meeting, Campaign+Content, Output+Shift. Expansion I should therefore deepen play patterns rather than invent replacement engines.
- IT is currently the deepest native Employee pool and does not need another generic low-cost body in the first expansion.

## Expansion I target
A first batch of **10 cards** is enough to test the loop: 2 Customer Service, 1 IT, 2 Office, 2 Marketing, 2 Production, 1 Neutral. Prioritize early bodies, reactive diversity, engine connectors and utility over new finishers.
